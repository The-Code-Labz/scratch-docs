Steak and egg Yum Yum bowls

Publix shopping haul ⬇️
3 lb top sirloin steak
1½ cups dry jasmine rice
7 Nellie’s eggs
32 oz liquid egg whites
16 oz Sugar Bombs tomatoes
8 Kraft Singles, or your favorite cheese — 50 calories per slice
450 g fat-free plain Greek yogurt
30 g no-sugar-added ketchup
10 g zero-calorie stevia sweetener
5 g rice vinegar

Seasonings needed ⬇️
Kinder’s The Blend — salt, pepper and garlic
Garlic powder
Paprika
Onion powder
Fine table salt

Stage 1 ⬇️
Add 1½ cups raw jasmine rice and 3 cups water to your rice cooker. Let it cook.

Stage 2 ⬇️
Chop up 3 lb top sirloin steak.
Season with 20 g Kinder’s The Blend.
Cook in your Cooking for Gains skillet.

Stage 3 ⬇️
Scramble 7 eggs with 32 oz liquid egg whites. Cook until fully set.

Stage 4 ⬇️
Make the Yum Yum sauce. Mix together:
450 g fat-free plain Greek yogurt
30 g no-sugar-added ketchup
10 g zero-calorie stevia sweetener
5 g rice vinegar
3 g garlic powder
3 g paprika
2 g onion powder
2 g fine table salt
Mix until smooth. No water needed.

Stage 5 ⬇️
While the steak and eggs cook, dice up 16 oz tomatoes.

Stage 6 ⬇️
Your steak is done. Add the following:
Cooked eggs
Cooked rice
Diced tomatoes
Yum Yum sauce
8 slices Kraft cheese, or your favorite cheese — 50 calories per slice
Mix everything together.

Stage 7 ⬇️
Divide into 7 meal-prep containers. Keep the portions within 20–30 g of each other.

540 calories | 70g protein | 34g carbs | 13g fat

STEAK & EGG BREAKFAST RICE ×7

PUBLIX SHOPPING HAUL ⬇️
4 Heritage shaved ribeye packs — 12 oz each
1 Publix jasmine rice bag — 907g
1 large egg carton — 12 count
1 Egg Beaters egg-white carton — 32 oz
1 Kraft fat-free cheddar bag — 7 oz
1 unsalted butter box — 16 oz
Garlic powder
Smoked paprika
Black pepper
Salt

STAGE 1: STEAK

3 lb raw shaved ribeye
8g garlic powder
4g smoked paprika
2g black pepper
4g salt
Season steak. Cook in batches on medium for 7–10 minutes, until browned and 165°F. Set aside.

STAGE 2: RICE

270g jasmine rice, weighed raw
Add water using your rice cooker’s directions. Cook, then fluff. This is the rice for all 7 meals.

STAGE 3: EGGS

7 large eggs
16 oz egg whites
¼ oz unsalted butter
Whisk eggs and egg whites. Melt butter on medium-low. Add eggs. Stir until fully set, 160°F. Set aside.

STAGE 4: MIX

7 oz fat-free cheddar
Return steak, rice and eggs to the skillet. Add cheese. Fold gently on low until softened.

STAGE 5: PORTION

Weigh the finished food without the pan. Divide evenly into 7 meals. Refrigerate within 2 hours. Keep 3–4 days in the fridge; freeze the rest.

REHEAT
Freezer: Add ¼ cup water. Cover with a microwave-safe paper towel. Microwave about 5 minutes.

Fridge: Add ⅛ cup water. Cover with a microwave-safe paper towel. Microwave about 2 minutes 30 seconds.

Using my CFG containers? Follow the container’s microwave and lid directions instead of using a paper towel.

Stir halfway. Add time until hot throughout.

## Recipe 3

Calories: ~455 per meal
Protein: 61g | Carbs: 43g | Fat: 4g

Smoky turkey chili ×7
7 meals

Publix shopping haul ⬇️
3 Jennie-O 99% lean ground turkey packs — 16 oz each
2 kidney bean cans — 15.5 oz each
1 black bean can — 15.25 oz
1 diced tomato can — 28 oz
1 tomato sauce can — 15 oz
1 unsalted butter box — 16 oz
Chili powder
Smoked paprika
Garlic powder
Ground cumin
Black pepper

Stage 1: Turkey ⬇️
3 lb raw ground turkey
½ oz unsalted butter
24g chili powder
8g smoked paprika
8g garlic powder
4g ground cumin
2g black pepper
Melt butter on medium. Add turkey and seasonings. Cook in batches. Break into small pieces. Cook to 165°F.

Stage 2: Add ⬇️
2 cans kidney beans, with liquid
1 can black beans, with liquid
1 can diced tomatoes, with juice
1 can tomato sauce
Return all turkey to the skillet. Add everything and stir.

Stage 3: Simmer ⬇️
Simmer uncovered on low for 20 minutes. Stir often. Add a splash of water if too thick.

Stage 4: Portion ⬇️
Weigh the finished chili without the pan. Divide into 7 equal meals. Refrigerate within 2 hours. Keep 3–4 days in the fridge; freeze the rest.

Reheat ⬇️
Freezer: Add ¼ cup water. Cover with a microwave-safe paper towel. Microwave about 5 minutes.

Fridge: Add ⅛ cup water. Cover with a microwave-safe paper towel. Microwave about 2 minutes 30 seconds.

Using my CFG containers? Follow the container’s microwave and lid directions instead of using a paper towel.

Stir halfway. Add time until hot throughout.

## Recipe 4

BLUEBERRY LEMON OAT MUFFINS 🫐🍋

MAKES 12 MUFFINS

PER MUFFIN — ESTIMATED
146 calories | 7g protein | 23g carbs | 3g fat

STAGE 1: GRIND THE OATS

1½ cups old-fashioned oats (120g)
Blend into a fine flour. Preheat the oven to 375°F.

STAGE 2: MIX THE DRY INGREDIENTS

All the ground oats from Stage 1
1 cup all-purpose flour (120g)
2 tsp baking powder (8g)
¼ tsp salt
Whisk together in a large bowl.

STAGE 3: WHISK THE WET INGREDIENTS

1½ cups plain nonfat Greek yogurt (340g)
2 large eggs
¼ cup honey (84g)
2 tbsp unsalted butter, melted (28g)
Let the melted butter cool slightly, then whisk everything together in a separate bowl.

STAGE 4: ADD THE LEMON

2 tbsp lemon juice (30g)
1 tbsp finely grated lemon zest (6g)
1 tsp pure vanilla extract
Stir into the wet mixture. Use only the yellow lemon skin when zesting.

STAGE 5: FOLD IN THE BERRIES

1 cup fresh blueberries (148g)
Rinse and dry the berries. Stir the wet mixture into the dry ingredients, then gently fold in the blueberries. Stop once no dry flour remains.

STAGE 6: PORTION

Line a standard 12-cup muffin pan with paper liners. Divide all the batter evenly between the 12 cups.

STAGE 7: BAKE & COOL

Bake at 375°F for about 22–28 minutes, until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out without wet batter.

Cool in the pan for 5 minutes, then transfer the muffins to a wire rack.

WHOLE BATCH — ESTIMATED
1,755 calories
80g protein
278g carbs
41g fat

## Recipe 5

STEAK FAJITA RICE BOWLS ×7

PER MEAL • ESTIMATED
650 calories | 56g protein | 60g carbs | 19g fat

PUBLIX SHOPPING HAUL

3 packs top sirloin steak — about 3 lb total
1 bag jasmine rice — 32 oz
2 red bell peppers
1 yellow bell pepper
1 large white onion
1 bag reduced-fat Mexican cheese — 8 oz
1 jar medium salsa — 16 oz
1 box unsalted butter — 16 oz
Chili powder
Garlic powder
Smoked paprika
Black pepper
Salt

STAGE 1: STEAK

3 lb top sirloin
½ oz unsalted butter
10g chili powder
8g garlic powder
6g smoked paprika
4g black pepper
6g salt
Slice the steak. Melt the butter. Cook in batches to 145°F. Rest for 3 minutes. Set aside.

STAGE 2: RICE

15 oz dry jasmine rice
Rinse the rice. Add water using your rice cooker’s directions. Cook and fluff.

STAGE 3: PEPPERS AND ONION

2 red bell peppers
1 yellow bell pepper
1 large white onion
¼ cup water
Slice everything. Cook on medium until tender. Set aside.

STAGE 4: MIX

16 oz medium salsa
8 oz reduced-fat Mexican cheese
Add the steak, rice, peppers, and onion to the skillet. Add the salsa and cheese. Fold together on low.

STAGE 5: PORTION

Divide everything evenly into 7 meals.

REHEAT

Freezer: Add ¼ cup water. Cover with a microwave-safe paper towel. Microwave about 5 minutes.

Fridge: Add ⅛ cup water. Cover with a microwave-safe paper towel. Microwave about 2 minutes 30 seconds.

Using my CFG containers? Follow the container’s microwave and lid directions instead of using a paper towel.

Stir halfway. Add time if needed until hot throughout.

Keep 3–4 days in the fridge. Freeze the rest.
